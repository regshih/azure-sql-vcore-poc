# Community conduct

Help others participate safely and productively.

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and respect differing experience.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate, or disclose someone’s
  private information.
- Do not post credentials, customer material, or unauthorized test targets.
- Respect a request to stop an unwelcome interaction.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or remove
access when necessary to protect the project and its participants. Decisions
should be proportionate, explainable where privacy permits, and open to a
respectful request for review.

Report concerns through an approved private maintainer channel. If no channel is
published, request one without describing private details publicly. Maintainers
must protect reporter privacy and address conflicts of interest when reviewing
a concern. Good-faith reports must not lead to retaliation.
